Pace Race

One real quote, one race. The pacer runs at the speed just past your own; beat it and the next race is one step faster, miss it and the same quote comes back for a rematch. Your best run on each quote becomes a ghost you race next time.

Word Turf

One board of 25 words, two players. Type a word to take its square, type your rival’s squares to steal them, and finish a row, column or diagonal for 3 more points and a seal nobody can break for 15 seconds. You see what each other is going for, live. Seventy-five seconds; most points wins.
